### Generating Shapes

We evaluate the quality of the PCA-based shape representations by generating synthetic spinal structures. Specifically, we impose a probabilistic model on the leading PCA coefficients, assuming independent zero-mean Gaussian distributions for the components required to explain 90% of the total variance. Model parameters are estimated from the training data. New shapes are then generated by sampling coefficients from this distribution and mapping them back to the object space via the PCA basis.

### Principal Component Shape Regression

We visualize deformations from mean shapes along coefficient directions, $\mu_{j} + \hat\beta_{j} V_{j}$ where $\hat\beta_{j}\in\mathbb{R}^{d}$ are the estimated regression coefficients and $V_{j}$ is a matrix of the top singular vectors of the $j^{th}$ object. These deformations highlight structural variations associated with a condition.
